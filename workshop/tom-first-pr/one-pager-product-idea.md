# Site Health Assistant

## The Problem

Most site owners publish and never come back. Over time, small issues pile up unnoticed: broken links, images that fail to load, stale info (hours, prices), declining SEO, and slower load times. Owners usually only find out when a customer complains or traffic quietly drops — by then it's too late.

## The Idea

An internal Wix feature that continuously scans published sites and surfaces health issues before they hurt the business. Instead of a dry technical report, the user gets a clear alert with one obvious action: "Auto-fix" or "View details."

## How It Works

1. **Automated scanning** — a bot checks every published site: links, images, load speed, SEO meta tags, form functionality.
2. **Health score** — a simple 0–100 score shown on the dashboard, with a trend over time.
3. **Targeted alerts** — only triggered on meaningful change, not constant noise. E.g., "3 links on your site point to a page that no longer exists."
4. **One-click fixes** — for simple issues (broken link, missing image), an instant fix button; for complex issues, a direct link to the relevant editor.

## Target Users

Small-to-medium site owners without a technical team who publish and rarely check back. Also agencies managing many client sites.

## Business Value

- **Retention**: a site that stays healthy and current reduces churn and complaints.
- **Monetization opportunity**: an "advanced auto-fix" tier could be a premium feature.
- **Competitive edge**: few competitors offer built-in proactive monitoring.

## Success Metrics

- Reduction in the share of sites scoring below 60.
- Alert open/action rate.
- Correlation between high health scores and lower churn.

## Next Step

Run a small pilot on an internal site sample, validate scan accuracy against manual review, then expand gradually.
